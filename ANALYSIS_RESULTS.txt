BITWISE OR BUG DETAILS (FIXED)
================================================================================

Location: src/hashes.c:2146
Commit: 118bd9db

Before:
  if (... || (opts_type & OPTS_TYPE_HASH_SPLIT) | (user_options->hash_copy == true))
                                                  ^
                                            single | (bitwise OR)

After:
  if (... || (opts_type & OPTS_TYPE_HASH_SPLIT) || (user_options->hash_copy == true))
                                                  ^^
                                            double || (logical OR)

Impact: 
  - Bitwise OR doesn't short-circuit, evaluates both sides
  - Logical OR short-circuits, skips right side if left is true
  - Result in this specific case: functionally equivalent (no side effects)
  - Semantic issue: incorrect operator for boolean logic
  - Performance issue: unnecessary evaluation

Status: ✅ FIXED, no remaining instances found
